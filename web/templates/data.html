Reading the figures

  • Amounts are whole Australian dollars.
  • Budget estimates and actuals are different series. Every amount carries its estimate type (Budget estimate, forward estimate, estimated actual or actual) and the document it came from.
  • Contract and grant values are whole-of-life values as published, not annual spending. The annual estimates files spread each value evenly over its term, and are estimates.
  • Program line items don’t add up to the whole-of-government total. The difference is the whole-of-government adjustments.
  • Columns marked AI come from a language model and may be wrong. See how contracts are classified and how suppliers are grouped.
  • Signals are patterns worth a closer look, not findings of wrongdoing.

Suppliers who are people

Some suppliers and grant recipients are individuals, such as sole traders. Their names are as the government published them. This site leaves them out of rankings and signal lists, and we ask that you do the same. The files flag them, and the methodology explains how they are identified.
