Worth a closer look

Patterns in contract notices

These rules pick out contract notices with patterns that deserve a second look: values that grew after award, late publication, values just under a procurement threshold, and repeated limited tenders to one supplier. Each rule is a fixed, measurable test applied to every contract published on AusTender. A match is not a finding of wrongdoing, and each rule lists common legitimate explanations.
