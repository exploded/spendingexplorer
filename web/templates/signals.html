Worth a closer look

Patterns in contract notices

These rules pick out contract notices with patterns that deserve a second look: values that grew after award, late publication (singly or in batches), values just under a procurement threshold, repeated limited tenders to one supplier, and limited tenders that grew past the threshold. Each rule is a fixed, measurable test applied to every contract published on AusTender. A match is not a finding of wrongdoing, and each rule lists common legitimate explanations.
